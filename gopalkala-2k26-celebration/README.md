# 🥣 Gopalkala 2K26 — Automatic Celebration

A cinematic one-page React + Vite festival experience for Gopalkala / Dahi Handi.

## ✨ Experience

1. Click **Start Celebration** once (required by browsers to start background audio).
2. Krishna-themed visuals appear in the background.
3. All 11 Gopalkala ingredients automatically fly into the decorated handi.
4. The handi mixes automatically with a spoon animation.
5. Four illustrated children enter from both sides.
6. They lift their bowls and eat the freshly prepared Gopalkala.
7. A festive final card reveals the generated Gopalkala celebration poster.

## 🥗 Ingredients

Poha, Chana Dal, Cucumber, Apple, Pomegranate, Corn, Cut Banana, Coconut, Coriander, Salt and Lemon.
