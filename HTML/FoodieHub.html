About Us

FoodieHub is a fictional food ordering website created for practicing HTML. It demonstrates how an online restaurant can display its menu and collect order details.

Why Choose FoodieHub?

  • A variety of delicious meals
  • Vegetarian and non-vegetarian options
  • Simple menu navigation
  • Easy-to-understand ordering form

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order?

Choose a food item, enter your details, and complete the sample order form.

Can I order multiple food items?

This HTML-only example allows one food item per submission. A real multi-item cart would require additional functionality.

Can I pay online?

The payment options are included for form practice only. No actual payment is processed.

Are these real prices?

No. The menu prices and offers are sample values for this learning project.
